# Admin User Manual

## 1. Access and responsibility

Only active staff with an allowed base role and exact permissions may enter protected ERP areas. Do not share accounts. Super Admin access is reserved for system administration. Departmental labels use the tested mapping documented in the role-permission guide.

## 2. Daily administration

Review dashboard alerts, overdue tasks, passport/visa/insurance expiry warnings, failed jobs, scheduler heartbeat, error incidents and backup status. Investigate warnings before processing sensitive operations.

## 3. Master data

Maintain countries, cities, airports, airlines, visa types, suppliers, agents, global hotels, Umrah hotels, insurance providers, chart of accounts and bank accounts. Codes and linked references must remain unique and internally consistent. Do not replace normalized references with free text when a master exists.

## 4. Travel operations

Create and maintain visa applications, flight bookings, Umrah packages and bookings, hotels and travel-insurance policies. Use customer and agent links correctly. Upload documents only to approved private-storage fields. Confirm totals, dates, status and linked payments before approval.

## 5. Accounting

Create manual vouchers as drafts. A posted voucher must balance before it can reach the general ledger. Use approved posting workflows for receipts, supplier transactions, refunds and insurance payments. Never edit posted accounting history to hide an error; use the linked reversal process.

Use the chart of accounts, bank master, reconciliation, trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet, cash/bank book and party statements. Respect accounting period locks.

## 6. HR and payroll

Maintain departments, designations, leave types, staff, attendance, documents, leave, ledgers, payroll and performance notes. HR Reports requires both HR staff visibility and report access through the central permission contract.

## 7. Website and support

Publish only reviewed content. Marketing deletion rights are explicit and should be used carefully. Support staff may manage customer inquiries and tickets within their documented bundle. Public inquiries and portal tickets may contain personal information and must not be exported casually.

## 8. Backups, restore and system health

Create and verify backups regularly. Review Google Drive upload status. Restore is not a normal troubleshooting step and requires the documented safety process. Use system health and error monitoring before escalating infrastructure problems.

## 9. Karwan AI

Keep external providers disabled unless separately approved. Public AI must not reveal private records. Staff answers must follow the signed-in user's permissions. Review usage metadata without storing unnecessary message content.

## 10. Audit discipline

Activity and login logs are evidence. Do not modify records outside approved ERP workflows. Report suspected access misuse, accounting imbalance, exposed files or secret leakage immediately.
