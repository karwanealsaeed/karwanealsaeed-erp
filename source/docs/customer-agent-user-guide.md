# Customer and Agent User Guide

## 1. Portal access

Use the customer/agent portal login supplied by Karwan E Al Saeed Travel & Tours. Keep your password private. The portal may end a session when an account is deactivated or when security settings change.

## 2. Profile and password

Review your name, contact information and other editable profile fields. Some identifiers are controlled by staff and cannot be changed from the portal. Use the password form to change your password; the current password is required.

## 3. Bookings and services

The portal shows only records linked to your account, including visa applications, flight bookings, Umrah bookings, hotel bookings, travel-insurance policies, invoices, vouchers and booking tracking. Contact support if a linked service is missing rather than creating a duplicate account.

## 4. Documents

Upload requested documents through the approved portal forms. Documents and payment receipts are stored privately and downloaded through protected routes. Never share a private download link or upload an unrelated person's identity document.

## 5. Payments

Payment uploads require supporting receipt evidence. A submitted payment is not automatically approved. Internal review and accounting posting determine the final status. Use the portal receipt link only for your own approved records.

## 6. Family members and notifications

Add only family members whose information you are authorized to provide. Review notifications for booking, document, payment and service updates. The portal prevents access to another customer's family, notifications, documents and tickets.

## 7. Support tickets

Create a support ticket with a clear subject and relevant details. Do not include card passwords, online-banking credentials or unrelated sensitive information. Replies remain linked to your own portal account.

## 8. Karwan AI

Portal AI may answer from public information and records that your account is authorized to access. It must not expose another customer, agent or staff record. For final travel, visa, fare or refund decisions, confirm with authorized staff.

## 9. Safety

Log out on shared devices. Report suspected unauthorized access immediately. Portal information is operational guidance and does not replace official airline, embassy, immigration or insurance decisions.
